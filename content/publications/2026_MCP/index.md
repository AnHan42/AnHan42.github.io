abstract: "The Model Context Protocol (MCP) standardizes interactions between AI agents and tools, enabling interoperability and accelerating the adoption of agentic AI systems. This creates a unique opportunity to embed trustworthiness directly at the protocol level. As an interface between agents and tools, MCP serves as a natural enforcement point, where improvements can propagate across entire ecosystems. Analyzing MCP through the European Commission’s Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI, we identify fundamental shifts in how trustworthiness manifests in agentic systems, the challenges these shifts introduce, and key intervention points. We argue that MCP’s architecture provides a strong foundation for trustworthy AI and propose practical enhancements to strengthen it, enabling more responsible and reliable deployments."
