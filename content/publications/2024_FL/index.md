abstract: "Machine learning on large-scale genomic or transcriptomic data is crucial for modern health applications such as precision medicine, where treatments are tailored to individual patients based on biomarkers and molecular data. However, such data is sensitive, distributed, and difficult to centralize due to privacy and regulatory constraints. Federated learning offers a solution by enabling decentralized model training without sharing raw data. In this work, we evaluate federated learning approaches on transcriptomic datasets, comparing frameworks such as TensorFlow Federated and Flower. We conduct experiments on disease prognosis and cell type classification tasks, analyzing model performance, communication costs, and system trade-offs."
