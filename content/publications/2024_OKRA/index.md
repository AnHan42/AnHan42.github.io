abstract: "Medical imaging plays a central role in modern healthcare, enabling applications from disease diagnosis to personalized treatment. Machine learning methods, particularly kernel methods, are widely used in this domain but face challenges due to data privacy constraints and the high dimensionality of medical images. Data is often distributed across institutions and cannot be centralized. Existing privacy-preserving approaches typically involve trade-offs between accuracy and efficiency. In this work, we introduce OKRA (Orthonormal K-fRAmes), a novel randomized encoding-based approach for efficient and privacy-preserving kernel learning on distributed medical image data. The method improves scalability while maintaining strong predictive performance."
