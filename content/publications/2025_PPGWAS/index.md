abstract: "Genome-wide association studies (GWAS) require large-scale data sharing across institutions, raising major privacy concerns. This work introduces PP-GWAS, a privacy-preserving framework enabling multi-site GWAS without exposing sensitive genomic data. By combining cryptographic techniques and distributed computation, the method ensures data confidentiality while maintaining analytical accuracy."
