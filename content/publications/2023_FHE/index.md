abstract: "Smart mobility is a promising approach to meet urban transport needs in an environmentally and user-friendly way. Smart mobility computes itineraries with multiple means of transportation, e.g., trams, rental bikes or electric scooters, according to customer preferences. A mobility platform cares for reservations, connecting transports, invoicing and billing. This requires sharing sensible personal data with multiple parties, and puts data privacy at risk. In this paper, we investigate if fully homomorphic encryption (FHE) can be applied in practice to mitigate such privacy issues. FHE allows to calculate on encrypted data, without having to decrypt it first. We implemented three typical distributed computations in a smart mobility scenario with SEAL, a recent programming library for FHE. With this implementation, we have measured memory consumption and execution times for three variants of distributed transactions."
