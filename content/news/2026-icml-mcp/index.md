From July 6-11, 2026, I am at ICML 2026 in Seoul, where Arjhun Swaminathan and I are presenting our position paper, "Position: Trustworthy Model Context Protocol Enables Responsible Agentic AI". We argue that the Model Context Protocol is a natural enforcement point for trustworthy AI, and propose practical enhancements to strengthen it across agentic systems. If you'd rather read a shorter, more accessible write-up first, SCRAI published a companion piece, "Let's build a Trustworthy Model Context Protocol!", covering the same ideas. Come find me at ICML if you want to talk about it!
