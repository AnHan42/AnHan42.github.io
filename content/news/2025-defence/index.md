In September 2025, I successfully defended my PhD thesis on Privacy-Preserving Distributed Learning: Applications on Healthcare Data at Leipzig University and ScaDS.AI (Center for Scalable Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence) Dresden/Leipzig. 
My thesis investigates methods for training machine learning models 
across distributed datasets without compromising patient privacy.
I am grateful to my supervisor Prof. Erik Buchmann and my co-supervisor Dr. Mete Akgün, all my colleagues, and everyone who supported me throughout this journey.
