On March 23, 2026, I gave a keynote titled "Engineering Responsible AI Systems"
at the SCRAI session "Building AI We Can Trust", part of the After The Algorithm
festival in Zurich. The session brought together business leaders, policymakers,
and AI builders to explore responsible AI across five lenses: business,
technology, legal, collaboration, and design. I was taking on the technical perspective discussing how to integrate RAI in the ML workflow. Feel free to reach out when you're interested in how to implement responsible AI practices into your lifecycle!
