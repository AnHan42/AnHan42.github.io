Recorded on October 30, 2024 and released in January 2025, this episode of the Arbeitskreis E-Learning's podcast series on AI in higher education looks at how universities can use AI tools responsibly in teaching. I discussed privacy and machine learning, and Dr. Hermann Diebel-Fischer (ScaDS.AI, TU Dresden) covered the ethical questions around algorithmic bias, transparency, and fairness.
