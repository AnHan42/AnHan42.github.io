In October 2025, I joined the Swiss Centre for Responsible AI (SCRAI) 
as Research Lead. SCRAI is a joint initiative of all cantonal universities 
of Zurich, funded by DIZH, with the mission of advancing responsible 
and trustworthy AI research across disciplines.
In this role, I will lead and coordinate research activities, 
bridging AI security, privacy, and governance across the participating institutions.
